UroLift recovery,
day by day

UroLift has the fastest recovery of any BPH procedure: an office visit, usually no catheter, and most men back at a desk within a day or two. Here is the honest shape of it, including the couple of weeks of irritation worth expecting.

The timeline

Short, mild, and front-loaded. Most men call it easier than the dentist.

01

Day 0 to 2: back to life

Home within the hour, usually without a catheter. Expect burning with urination and some urgency; desk work is realistic the next day, and flow often improves immediately.

02

Weeks 1 to 2: the irritation window

Burning, urgency, some pelvic ache, occasionally a little blood in the urine, all typical while the implants settle. Simple medication keeps it manageable.

03

Weeks 2 to 4: settled

Irritation fades, the improved stream stays, and life is back to normal. Symptom scores get rechecked to confirm the win.

Common questions about UroLift recovery

When can I go back to work after UroLift

Desk work within a day or two for most men. Physically demanding jobs usually wait about a week. There is no incision to protect, so the limiter is comfort, not healing.

When can I have sex after UroLift

Typically within a week or two, whenever comfortable. UroLift's pivotal trial reported no new sustained ejaculatory or erectile dysfunction, so there is nothing structural to wait on.

Will I need a catheter

Usually not. Most men leave the office without one, which is one of UroLift's defining advantages over surgical options.

How long does UroLift recovery take

Most men are back at work in two to three days, the burning and urgency of the first one to two weeks settle by the end of week two, and the full result is evident by about four weeks. There is usually no catheter and no restriction on sex beyond comfort.
