How coverage works

Three things decide your number, and the office checks all three before you commit.

01

Medicare

Covers office-based UroLift for documented BPH. After the Part B deductible, Medicare pays 80 percent, and a supplement typically absorbs most or all of the rest.

02

PPO and commercial plans

Most cover UroLift with documented symptoms; some require prior authorization, which the office handles, including the imaging and symptom-score paperwork.

03

No facility markup

No hospital admission, no operating room, no anesthesia team. The office setting removes the largest line items a surgical bill usually carries.

What patients actually pay

UroLift is an office procedure, which is the single biggest reason the bill runs lighter than hospital surgery: there is no facility or anesthesia charge, only the procedure and the implants, billed under your medical benefit. Common questions about UroLift costs

Does Medicare cover UroLift

Yes, for documented BPH symptoms. After the Part B deductible, Medicare pays 80 percent, and a Medicare supplement typically covers most or all of the remainder, which is why many men with a supplement owe little to nothing.

What will I actually pay out of pocket

It depends on your deductible status, your coinsurance percentage, and whether you carry a supplement, not on a list price. The office verifies your specific benefits and tells you the real number before anything is scheduled.

Is UroLift cheaper than surgery

Structurally, yes: no hospital, no operating room, no overnight stay. Your personal cost still depends on your plan, which is exactly what the verification sorts out in advance.

How much does UroLift cost

With Original Medicare, the $283 Part B deductible if it is not yet met, then 20 percent of the Medicare-allowed amount for the procedure and the implants, and nothing for a hospital because there is none. A Medigap supplement usually absorbs the coinsurance. PPO plans apply their deductible and coinsurance, and self-pay patients get one bundled quote with the implants included.
