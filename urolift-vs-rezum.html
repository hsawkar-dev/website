Common questions

Which works faster, UroLift or Rezum

UroLift, by months. The implants hold tissue open immediately, and most men feel the difference within the first week, usually with no catheter. Rezūm's relief builds over three to six months as the treated tissue is reabsorbed, with a short-term catheter at the start.

Which lasts longer

Both carry five-year data. Rezūm's surgical retreatment rate, roughly one in twenty by year five, is among the lowest of any office option; UroLift's is roughly one in seven. Rezūm also handles a median lobe more reliably, which affects durability in that anatomy.

Which is better for sexual function

UroLift holds the cleanest record: no new, sustained ejaculatory or erectile dysfunction in its pivotal trial. Rezūm preserved erections and ejaculation for the large majority as well, at slightly lower rates. If ejaculation is your single deciding factor, UroLift usually wins.

What if my prostate is too big for either

Both are designed for roughly 30 to 80 cc. Above that range, PAE and Aquablation take over, and Dr. Sawkar offers both, so nobody has to force a small-gland tool onto a large-gland problem.
