Treatments/Enlarged prostate/PAE vs Rezūm

PAE or Rezūm:
the honest comparison

Both shrink the prostate rather than cutting it out, and both keep you out of the operating room, but they get there by opposite routes: PAE starves the gland of blood so it shrinks on its own, Rezūm destroys targeted tissue with steam and lets the body clear it. Dr. Sawkar offers both: he performs Rezūm in the office and leads prostate artery embolization care, with the embolization performed by an interventional radiologist, so this comparison has no thumb on the scale.

Side by side

Two routes to a smaller gland. PAE starves the excess tissue from the inside, through the arteries; Rezūm destroys it with brief bursts of steam delivered through the urethra. Neither one cuts, and both preserve sexual function far better than traditional surgery.

How men actually choose

01

Choose Rezūm when the gland is small to medium

You want a single office visit under local anesthetic, a result that held up well over five years in the trial, and you can live with a catheter for several days plus a few weeks of burning and urgency while the treated tissue clears. For a gland of roughly 30 to 80 cc, that is a fair trade, and many men take it.

02

Choose PAE when the prostate is large or the catheter is a deal-breaker

You want to skip the catheter entirely, you take blood thinners you would rather not interrupt, or the prostate is bigger than Rezūm handles well. PAE has no practical size ceiling, recovery is a day or two, and it also fits men whose anatomy sits outside Rezūm's range for any other reason.

03

Let the measurement referee

Gland volume, shape, median lobe, bladder health, and your medical history all weigh in. A quick ultrasound turns this page's generalities into your specific answer, and often the volume alone settles it: under about 80 cc both are on the table, above it PAE is the one that applies.

Common questions

Which is more effective, PAE or Rezūm

In the size range where both apply, roughly 30 to 80 cc, symptom relief is similar and both build over weeks. Above 80 cc PAE is the only one of the two that applies. Rezūm has the better retreatment record in its trial; PAE has the lighter recovery and no catheter.

Which has the easier recovery

PAE. Most men are back to routine in a day or two with no catheter. Rezūm means a catheter for several days and a few weeks of burning and urgency while the treated tissue is reabsorbed.

Can I have Rezūm first and PAE later

Yes, in either order. Neither removes tissue or changes the anatomy in a way that blocks the other, so starting with one leaves the other fully available.

Does either affect sexual function

Erections are preserved with both. Ejaculation is preserved in the large majority with both, a major difference from TURP, where roughly 2 in 3 men lose it.
