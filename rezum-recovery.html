The timeline

The payoff is real and durable; it is just not immediate. Patience is part of the prescription.

01

Days 1 to 7: the catheter week

A soft catheter typically stays three to seven days while the treated tissue swells and settles. The least pleasant part, and temporary. Desk work is realistic within a few days.

02

Weeks 2 to 4: irritation fades

Urgency, frequency, and burning taper as swelling resolves. Many men notice the first genuine improvement in stream near the end of this window.

03

Months 2 to 6: the payoff

The body finishes reabsorbing treated tissue, the prostate shrinks, and symptom scores typically fall by half or more, holding for years in five-year data.

Common questions about Rezum recovery

How long is the catheter in after Rezum

Usually three to seven days, removed in a quick office visit. Men who dread the catheter most often end up calling it an inconvenience rather than an ordeal.

When will Rezum start working

First improvement commonly appears around weeks two to four, with steady gains after. The full result arrives at three to six months, once the treated tissue is reabsorbed.

When can I have sex after Rezum

Once comfortable, typically after one to two weeks. Erections were unaffected in the five-year trial and ejaculation preserved for the large majority.
